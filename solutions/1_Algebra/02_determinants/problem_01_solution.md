## Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

### Compute:
$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix} \quad \text{and} \quad \det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix}
$$

### Solution:

**1. First Matrix Calculation:**
$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = (3)(4) - (-2)(5) = 12 + 10 = 22
$$

**2. Second Matrix Calculation:**
$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix} = (1)(14) - (7)(2) = 14 - 14 = 0
$$

### Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

* **Matrix 1** is **invertible** because its determinant is $22$ (which is non-zero).
* **Matrix 2** is **not invertible** because its determinant is $0$.
