## Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2x2 Matrix

### Compute:
$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix}
$$

### Solution:

1. **First Matrix:**
   $$
   \det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = (3)(4) - (-2)(5) = 12 + 10 = 22
   $$

2. **Second Matrix:**
   $$
   \det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix} = (1)(14) - (7)(2) = 14 - 14 = 0
   $$

### Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.
* **Matrix 1 $\begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \\ 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix}$ is invertible** because its determinant ($22$) is non-zero.
* **Matrix 2 $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \\ 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix}$ is not invertible** because its determinant is $0$ (its columns/rows are linearly dependent).
