### Exercise 4. Parabola

**Statement:**  
For

$$
y^2 = 12x,
$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $x = 3$ and use them to make a sketch.

> **Why this exercise:** teaches how to move from the equation of a parabola to its basic geometry.

---

### Solution

#### 1. Identify Geometric Properties
The canonical equation for a parabola opening horizontally is $y^2 = 4px$ (or $y^2 = 2px$):

- **Vertex:** $(0, 0)$
- **Axis of Symmetry:** The $x$-axis ($y = 0$), because the equation is symmetric with respect to $y$ (replacing $y$ with $-y$ leaves the equation unchanged).
- **Opening Direction:** Opens to the **right** (along the positive $x$-axis), because the coefficient of $x$ ($12$) is positive.
- **Focal Parameter:** $4p = 12 \implies p = 3$, meaning the focus is at $(3, 0)$ and the directrix is $x = -3$.

---

#### 2. Find Points for $x = 3$
Substitute $x = 3$ into the equation:

$$
y^2 = 12(3) = 36
$$

$$
y = \pm \sqrt{36} = \pm 6
$$

The two points on the parabola corresponding to $x = 3$ are **$(3, 6)$** and **$(3, -6)$**. (These points define the ends of the *latus rectum* segment passing through the focus).

---

#### 3. Sketch Description
- **Vertex:** Starts at the origin $(0, 0)$.
- **Key Points:** Passes through $(3, 6)$ in the first quadrant and $(3, -6)$ in the fourth quadrant.
- **Shape:** A smooth, symmetric U-shaped curve opening to the right around the $x$-axis, growing wider as $x$ increases.
